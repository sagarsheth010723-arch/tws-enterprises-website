PROJECT OVERVIEW

Product thinking connected to business operations.

An internal web tool for controlled contact workflows, Telegram-user checks and channel operations.

This case study reflects the project’s confirmed scope and current development stage. It does not claim features or outcomes that have not yet been completed or publicly launched.

01 · THE CHALLENGE

Translate a real operational need into a clear digital product.

The product required connected user journeys, reliable data handling and administration without making the experience unnecessarily complex.

02 · OUR SOLUTION

A modular product structure connecting users, data and operations.

TWS ONE defined the workflow, interface system, backend structure and operational controls required for the current product stage.

03 · CURRENT OUTCOME

A product foundation aligned with the next development milestone.

The result is an active or in-development system with a clear roadmap for testing, deployment, operations and future improvements.

KEY PRODUCT CAPABILITIES

Designed around practical use cases.

01

Admin-Only Access

Restricted Google authentication for internal operations.

02

Contact Upload

Structured manual contact upload and selection workflow.

03

04

Cloud Functions

Server-side logic for secure integration workflows.

05

Operational Logs

Track processing, errors and status changes.
